Board: SW3 = 1 (panel on the on-board USB-UART instead of rlink, console on the JC cable); the board must not be attached to WSL. HALT and START are armed by the first click and done by a second click within 3 s. EXAM / DEP / LOAD ADRS work while halted. Knobs: click a position, or click the knob (right click: back). Switches: click; Shift+click clears the octal digit.
